# Stack page

A stack opens as a page of its own, a place to work in. Open it by clicking a stack in the [[library]] tree, from
Home, or from a breadcrumb.

## The header

- **Breadcrumb**: **All items**, then the stacks this one sits inside. Click one to go there.
- **Title**: the stack's name.
- **Status**: a menu with the stack's status. Choose another to change it. Setting a stack to Complete or On hold
  takes it and everything inside it out of the schedule. See [[statuses|Item statuses]].
- **Details**: opens the stack on the [[item-page|item page]], where you edit its description, priority,
  difficulty, subjects and tags.
- **New item**: opens the Create dialog. See [[create-dialog]].
- **Import or export…**: opens the import and export dialog for this stack. See [[import-export]].
- **Import…**: shown when an extension offers an importer or a card type.
- **Start rehearsal**: opens the run setup with this stack ticked. See [[rehearse]].

Under the header a line says how many of the stack's items, sub-stacks included, are in each status.

## Tabs

The tabs are Summary, List, Backlog, Roadmap, Board, Calendar, Reports, Sub-stacks and Goals. Tabs that do not fit
the window go into a **More** menu. The **Left** and **Right** arrow keys switch tabs. The last tab you used is
remembered for each stack.

### Summary

Read when the tab opens:

- four numbers for the last and next seven days: items completed, updated, created and due soon;
- **Status overview**: a ring with the total in the middle and every status with its count and percent;
- **Priorities**: a bar for each priority with its count;
- **Types**: how many notes, cards and questions;
- **Activity**: recent changes to the stack's items (created, edited, rehearsed), newest first;
- **Sub-stacks**: each with status, progress (done of total) and familiarity.

### List

The stack's items as a list, with the same search, columns, filters and bulk changes as the [[library]]. Above it
are the stack's due date (**Set due date** or **Change due date**), **Edit stack** and buttons for the stacks
inside. Below it, under **PDF files**, are the stack's PDFs with their page counts, **Add PDF ...** and
**Rename**. See [[pdf]].

### Backlog

Two lists: **Up next** and **Not yet planned**, each with the count per status. Drag an item between them, or use
its button. Moving an item to Up next makes it Learning; moving it to Not yet planned puts it On hold.

### Roadmap

A row for each stack inside this one and each item directly in it, with a bar from its first date to its next one on
a time line. A line marks today. **Zoom** offers Today, Weeks, Months and Quarters; scroll both ways. Each bar is a
button that opens its stack or item.

### Board

A column for each status. Drag an item to another column, or use its **Move** menu, to change its status. This
board is about statuses; the [[board|Board]] screen in the sidebar is a separate, free-form task board.

### Calendar

The [[calendar]] for this stack only: its sessions, deadlines and planned reviews.

### Reports

The stack's [[familiarity]] and the items it knows least. **Open all reports** goes to the [[reports]] screen.

### Sub-stacks

The tree of stacks inside this one, with status, progress, due date and familiarity. **Fold** and **Unfold** open
and close a branch; click a stack to open its page.

### Goals

What you want to reach with this stack, as a Markdown bullet list. Check boxes (`- [ ]`) are fine. Press
**Save goals** to save, or **Discard changes**. The goals are saved under a `## Goals` heading in the stack's
`_stack.md` file, so they are searchable and part of backups. The rest of the file is kept.
