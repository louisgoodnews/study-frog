# Why StudyFrog

StudyFrog is a learning app for your Linux desktop. It turns a folder of Markdown notes into a complete place to
study: linked notes, flashcards, spaced repetition, a calendar, a board and reports on how you are doing.

## Who it is for

StudyFrog is for people who learn on their own: students, researchers, people preparing for an exam or a
certificate, language learners. It suits you best if you already like writing notes in plain text, want your
reviews planned by a method that is backed by research, and want to keep your data on your own computer.

## What it does

- **Notes that link.** Write notes in Markdown and link them with `[[wikilinks]]`. The [[graph]] shows how they
  connect.
- **Cards and questions.** A card has a front and a back. A question has a prompt and a reference answer that
  you answer in your own words. See [[create-dialog|Creating items]].
- **Spaced repetition.** StudyFrog plans when you should see each item again, so you review just before you
  would forget. See [[familiarity|Familiarity and scheduling]].
- **Rehearsal your way.** Run through due items, everything in order, the hardest first, or the least familiar
  first, with or without a clock. See [[rehearse]].
- **Planning.** A [[calendar]] for study sessions, exams and deadlines, and a [[board]] for tasks.
- **Insight.** [[reports|Reports]] on what you remember, how fast you answer and what is coming up, plus
  [[achievements]] to keep you going.
- **PDFs.** Read, mark up and take notes from PDF files in the same folder. See [[pdf]].
- **An optional assistant.** Connect an AI model if you want one. It only proposes changes; you decide. See
  [[assistant]].

## The ideas behind it

1. **Your files, your data.** Everything you write is a plain Markdown file in a folder you choose. You can read
   and edit it without StudyFrog. The app's database is an index and a history, never the only copy of your
   knowledge.
2. **Local first.** There is no account and no cloud. StudyFrog works offline. Nothing leaves your computer
   unless you connect a remote AI model and allow it. See [[privacy]].
3. **Evidence-based.** Scheduling and the ways to practise follow published learning research.
4. **Measured.** How long you take to answer and how well you remember are recorded and used, for example to
   suggest a rating or to forecast your workload.
5. **Fast.** Starting, rehearsing and drawing the graph are kept quick, also with thousands of items.

## What StudyFrog does not do

- There is no cloud sync and no account. If you want your notes on several computers, sync the folder yourself
  (for example with a sync tool you already use).
- There is no mobile, Windows or Mac version yet. StudyFrog is for Linux.
- There is no real-time collaboration. StudyFrog has one user per profile.
- Typed answers are compared by similarity, not checked for truth. See the [[faq]].
