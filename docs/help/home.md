# Home

Home is the screen a vault opens on. It answers one question: what should I do now? Open it from the sidebar or by
clicking **StudyFrog** in the top bar.

## At the top

- A greeting (Good morning, Good afternoon or Good evening) and the number of stacks.
- **Items ready for today**: the items that are due or new and waiting.
- **Streak**: how many days in a row you have reviewed something.
- **Reviews today**: how many reviews you have done today.
- **Items in your vault**: how many cards, notes and questions there are.

## Start today's rehearsal

**Start today's rehearsal** starts a spaced-repetition run over everything that is ready, in all stacks. If nothing
is ready, the button is replaced by **Practice something else**, which opens the run setup. See [[rehearse]].

Stacks whose due date has passed are left out of today's rehearsal and the counts. You can still choose such a
stack by hand in the run setup.

## Needs attention

This card lists the five items you know least, and why:

- low [[familiarity]],
- overdue,
- an exam of its stack within 14 days.

Click an item to open it.

## Due now

The left column lists the items that are due, with how many days overdue each one is, and the number of new items.
If nothing is overdue it says so ("You are all caught up."). Click a title to open the item.

Under the list are the stacks with items waiting, each with its number of due and new items and a button
**Rehearse** followed by the stack's name, to study just that stack.

## Coming up

- **Today**: the study sessions planned for today, with their times and stacks. If there are none, Home suggests
  planning one in the [[calendar]].
- **Next 7 days**: the exams, tasks, appointments and deadlines of the next week from the calendar.

## Recent activity

- **Recently edited**: the items you changed last. Click one to open it.
- **Recent runs**: your last rehearsals.

Below the columns, Home lists the PDF files that changed most recently, to open them again. See [[pdf]].

## When Home is up to date

Home reads its numbers when you open it (and when you open the library). If you just changed something, open Home
again to see it.
