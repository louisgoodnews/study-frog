# Graph

The graph shows how your items link to each other. Every item is a dot; every `[[wikilink]]` between two items is a
line. Items that link a lot pull together, so groups of related notes become visible. Open **Graph** in the sidebar.

## Using the graph

- **Drag** the background to move around (pan).
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out. Titles appear next to the dots when you are zoomed in far enough.
- **Point** at a dot to see its title ("Pointing at: ...").
- **Click** a dot to open the item on its [[item-page|item page]].
- **Re-run layout** arranges the dots again from scratch.

The heading says how many items and links there are. The graph updates by itself when you add or change links; the
dots you know stay where they were.

## Items as a list

Open **Items as a list** under the graph to see the items as clickable titles. This works from the keyboard and with
a screen reader. The list shows the first items; use the [[library]] or the search in the top bar for the rest.

## Making links

Write `[[Title]]` in a note, card or question to link to the item with that title. You can also write
`[[Title|other words]]` to show other words, or `[[Title#Heading]]` to point at a heading. The **Link to a note**
button in the editor's toolbar inserts one. See [[item-page|Item page and editor]].

The item page lists **Links to** (where the item points) and **Linked from** (what points to it).

Links are matched by the item's title only. If you rename an item, links that use the old title no longer find it;
change them too.

## Speed

The graph stays smooth with thousands of items: 5 000 items with 10 000 links are laid out in about 4 ms per step.
