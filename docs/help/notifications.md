# Notifications

StudyFrog tells you what happened in three places: the status bar at the bottom, short pop-up messages, and the
notification center under the bell in the top bar.

## The status bar

The bar at the bottom of the window shows the latest message, for example "Saved." or an error in red, with a
**Dismiss** button. When there is no message it shows the size of your vault ("12 stacks · 340 items").

## Pop-up messages

Calendar reminders appear as a small card at the top right of the window. Click it to open the calendar, or press
**Dismiss notification** to close it. See [[calendar]].

## The notification center

Click the **bell** in the top bar to open it. The bell shows how many notifications you have not read yet.

- Every message StudyFrog shows is kept here: saves, imports, errors, unlocked [[achievements]], reminders.
- The list is grouped under **Today**, **Yesterday** and older dates, newest first.
- **Show**: **All**, **Unread** or **Errors**.
- **Mark read** or **Mark unread** on one notification; **Mark all read** for all of them.
- **Delete** one; **Clear all** removes every notification.
- A notification that has a target is a link. A reminder opens the calendar; a notification about an item opens its
  page.

Opening the panel does not mark anything as read. You decide.

Press **Esc** or click outside the panel to close it.

## How long notifications are kept

Notifications are kept in the vault's database, so they survive a restart. Each vault has its own. They are kept
for 30 days, and at most 200; older ones are removed.

## Reminders only while StudyFrog runs

A reminder shows once, at its time, while StudyFrog is open. StudyFrog has no background service, so it cannot
remind you while it is closed. A reminder missed that way still shows once when you open StudyFrog again, as long
as the entry it belongs to has not ended yet. See the [[faq]].
