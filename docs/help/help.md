# Using help

The help screen holds the pages you are reading now, and pages you write yourself. This page explains how to find
your way around it.

## Opening help

- The **?** button (**Help**) in the top bar opens the page for the screen you are on.
- The help button in the status bar at the bottom does the same.
- **F1** opens the page for the current screen from anywhere.

For example, on the Calendar it opens [[calendar]], on a stack's page [[stack-page]], and on the welcome page
[[getting-started|Getting started]]. **Back to** returns you to the screen you came from.

## Finding a page

The page tree on the left has four sections:

- **Start**: [[why-studyfrog|Why StudyFrog]], [[getting-started|Getting started]] and [[privacy|Privacy and your
  data]].
- **Screens**: one page for every screen, from [[home]] to [[notifications]].
- **Reference**: the [[faq]], the [[glossary]], [[keyboard|keyboard shortcuts]], [[query-language|the query
  language]], [[familiarity]] and [[statuses|item statuses]].
- **Your pages**: the pages you wrote.

**Search help** looks through the titles and the text of every page, yours included. Titles that match come first.

Links in a page take you to another page. Use **Back to** to return.

## Supplied pages and your own pages

The pages that come with StudyFrog are **read-only**. They change with new versions of the app. If you want to add
your own notes to one, press **Copy to my pages**: you get a copy that is yours to change.

Your own pages:

- are plain Markdown files in the `wiki/` folder of your vault,
- are part of your backups, like everything else in the vault,
- show up in the search,
- can link to the supplied pages by their id, for example `[[library]]` or `[[rehearse|how I rehearse]]`, and to
  each other by file name.

Press **New page** to write one: give it a **Page name** and a file name (without `.md`), write the **Page text** and
press **Save page**. **Edit** changes a page of yours; **Delete page** removes it after asking. You need an open
vault to keep pages of your own.

Your pages are not items: they are not rehearsed and do not show in the library.

## The assistant and your pages

The [[assistant]] can propose a new page of your own, or an edit to one. Like every proposal of the assistant, it
changes nothing until you accept and apply it.

## The First steps tutorial

The first time a vault opens, StudyFrog offers the **First steps** tutorial. It walks you through four steps:

1. create a stack,
2. create a card,
3. rehearse it,
4. plan a session in the calendar.

Each step opens the screen it talks about and is done when you have done it. You can skip the tutorial. It is
offered only once, but you can start it again any time from the help screen.
