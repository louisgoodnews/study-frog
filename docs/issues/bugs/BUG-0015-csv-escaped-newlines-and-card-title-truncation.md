## Context
After importing CSV flashcards generated by LLMs (such as the `psychologische-diagnostik` dataset), two issues appeared in the application:
1. Literal `\n` sequences inside CSV fields (e.g. `1. Akkurate Beschreibung...\n2. Prüfung...\n3. ...`) were written verbatim as `\n` instead of being converted to newline characters, causing numbered lists and multi-line answers to render on a single line with literal `\n` characters.
2. Card titles derived automatically from the `front` field (when the CSV `title` column is empty) were truncated at 60 characters (`line.chars().take(60).collect()`) by `default_card_title` in `crates/studyfrog-service/src/create.rs`, cutting off card titles mid-word after ~7–9 tokens across the library table, item editor, and rehearsal review list.

## Description
Root cause:
- Missing `\r\n` / `\n` unescaping in `examples/studyfrog-ext-example/src/delimited.rs` and `crates/studyfrog-service/src/xfer_csv_map.rs`.
- Redundant `.chars().take(60).collect()` in `crates/studyfrog-service/src/create.rs::default_card_title` (filename length was already bounded by `slug`).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Escaped newline sequences (\r\n and \n outside inline/display LaTeX $...$) in CSV/TSV imports and card content are converted to actual newline characters so multi-line text and ordered lists render properly
- [x] default_card_title preserves the full first non-empty line of the card front without truncating at 60 characters, and existing cards in a vault with 60-character truncated titles or literal \n sequences are automatically repaired on vault open

## Design Notes
- Unescape `\r\n` and `\n` (outside `$...$` math spans) in `examples/studyfrog-ext-example/src/delimited.rs`, `crates/studyfrog-service/src/xfer_csv_map.rs`, and `crates/studyfrog-service/src/content.rs`.
- Remove the 60-character truncation in `crates/studyfrog-service/src/create.rs::default_card_title` and widen the initial `Title` column in `crates/studyfrog-ui/src/view/library_table.rs`.
- Automatically repair existing cards on `open_vault` whose titles were truncated at 60 characters or whose fields contain literal `\n` sequences.
